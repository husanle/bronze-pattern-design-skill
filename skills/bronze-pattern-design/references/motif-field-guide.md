# 纹样识读 / Motif Field Guide

这是一张设计用形态速查表，不是断代或鉴定手册。术语的英文用于项目沟通；已有展签或馆藏英文名时，优先保留其原文。  
This is a visual reference for design, not an authentication or dating guide. The English terms are working labels; prefer the museum's own wording where available.

| 类别 / Motif | 看什么 / Visual cues | 产品试用 / Where to test |
|---|---|---|
| 兽面纹 / Animal mask | 中轴、双目、角和口部的对应关系。<br>Central axis, paired eyes, corresponding horns and mouth shapes. | 有明确正面的较宽版位。<br>A broad surface with a defined front. |
| 龙纹 / Dragon motif | 有头部线索的延伸、回转或盘曲结构；不要自动套用现代龙头。<br>An elongated, turning, or coiled structure with a head cue; avoid imposing a modern dragon head. | 顺轴使用，如细长侧面或包装腰带。<br>Along an axis, such as a long side or a packaging band. |
| 鸟纹 / Bird motif | 侧向眼、喙、翅尾；依展签判断是否细分。<br>Profile eye, beak, wing and tail; follow the label for a finer classification. | 单枚或交替边带。<br>Single mark or alternating border. |
| 凤鸟纹 / Phoenix-type bird motif | 冠、曲颈、层叠或卷曲尾羽；保留侧向和尾部节奏。<br>Crest, curved neck, layered or curling tail; preserve orientation and tail rhythm. | 封面或大面单枚。<br>A single motif on a cover or broad face. |
| 动物纹 / Animal motif | 轮廓、行进方向、鱼鳞或肢体等可辨细节；勿强确定物种。<br>Silhouette, direction, visible scales or limbs; avoid unsupported species IDs. | 连排行列、标签或窄边饰。<br>Procession, label, or narrow border. |
| 变形兽体纹 / Stylized animal motif | 折回、拉伸和卷钩的结构；与普通几何卷线区分时需看展签及上下文。<br>Foldbacks, elongated forms and hooks; consult the label and context before distinguishing it from a geometric scroll. | 连续带；可另做展平的生产稿。<br>Continuous bands; make a separate flattened artwork if needed. |
| 火纹 / Flame motif | 可见旋钩、中心与外围关系；并非所有圆形旋纹都是火纹。<br>Visible rotating hooks and center–rim relationship; not every round spiral is a flame motif. | 小型圆章、套标或局部压印。<br>Small seal, sleeve mark, or emboss. |
| 雷纹 / Thunder motif | 常见角形回旋；以来源具体叫法为准，不把所有折线都归成雷纹。<br>Often angular coils; do not classify every zigzag as a thunder motif. | 低对比底纹或细边。<br>Low-contrast ground or thin trim. |
| 目纹 / Eye motif | 眼框与瞳点是否本身反复出现，而非只是兽面的一部分。<br>Whether eye rims and pupils recur as units rather than appearing only within a mask. | 小章、标签或重复单元。<br>Stamp, label, or repeat unit. |
| 鳞纹 / Scale motif | 单元的重叠、错排与朝向；也可能是规整长环。<br>Overlap, stagger, orientation; some labeled examples use elongated loops. | 织物小块平铺或短边带。<br>Small textile repeat or short border. |
| 波曲纹 / Wave motif | 波峰、波谷、并行线及间填小形。<br>Crests, troughs, parallel lines, and intervening shapes. | 连续细边，避开功能刻度。<br>Continuous trim away from functional markings. |
| 几何纹 / Geometric motif | 连珠、网格、菱形等单元以及间距。<br>Beads, grids, lozenges, and their spacing. | 点引、定位、规则边带。<br>Dot guides, alignment, or regular borders. |
| 画像纹 / Figural scene | 人物或情节的连续关系；不从残片补认剧情。<br>Relationships between figures or scenes; do not infer a narrative from fragments. | 横向叙事面；不适合把单个角色随意镜像重复。<br>A horizontal narrative surface; avoid arbitrarily mirroring single figures. |

## 证据优先 / Evidence first

- **原文与推测分列。** 有馆名、展厅、器名或藏品号就照录；缺项留空。保留原照片名或链接与裁切位置在工作记录里。只有预览图时，可写“疑似兽面纹：可见中轴和双眼，角部遮挡”。  
  **Separate labels and inference.** Transcribe institution, gallery, object and accession details when available; leave gaps rather than filling them. Keep the file name or URL and crop position in working records. With only a limited photo, use wording like “possible animal mask: axis and paired eyes visible; horns obscured.”
- **对照同一物件上的多个层级。** 把贴边连珠与主体兽面分别记录；不要因为同处一块拓片就当成一种纹样。先判断是否能矫正拍摄倾斜、曲面或反光，必要时让它们留在来源局部中。  
  **Read ornament at different levels.** Record a bead border separately from a principal animal mask. Distinguish camera perspective, vessel curvature and glare from the ornament itself.
